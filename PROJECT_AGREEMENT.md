# Project Agreement: Oaktree Wealth Management Platform

**Client:** OaktreeFSG  
**Provider:** Tunga  
**Date:** February 16, 2026  
**Project Duration:** 4 Weeks (Accelerated MVP)

## 1. Executive Summary

Tunga will design and build a custom wealth management "Operating System" for Oaktree, streamlining the transition from public lead capture to internal operations and client self-service.

### Key Outcomes
*   **Centralized CRM:** Eliminate fragmented data; manage leads to clients in one flow.
*   **Rapid Reporting:** Search client → View status → Generate PDF report in seconds.
*   **Self-Service Experience:** Reduce administrative overhead with a secure client vault.
*   **Event Automation:** Direct integration between registrations and CRM activity logs.

## 2. Detailed Deliverables & Scope

### Phase 1: Public Website
The public-facing website serves as the primary digital storefront and lead generation tool for Oaktree, designed to inform visitors about the company's value proposition and guide them toward engagement.

*   **Home:** The main landing page designed to make a strong first impression. Features a compelling hero section, overview of core services, company process outline, value proposition, and client testimonials to build trust.
*   **About:** Provides a deeper understanding of Oaktree's identity, history, and mission. Communicates company values, introduces key team members, and builds a personal connection by sharing the brand story.
*   **Solutions:** The main hub for service offerings, giving a high-level overview of different operational areas. Users can navigate to detailed sub-pages from here.
    *   **Investment Services:** Delves into Oaktree's investment offerings, educating potential clients on strategies, opportunities, and the firm's expertise in asset management.
    *   **Real Estate Services:** Outlines capabilities in the real estate sector, detailing services like property management, investment consulting, and development advisory.
    *   **Consulting & Advisory:** Focuses on strategic guidance for businesses or individuals, highlighting expertise in professional advice, strategic planning, and problem-solving.
    *   **Insurance & Pension:** Addresses risk management and retirement planning needs, detailing insurance products and pension advisory services for long-term security.
*   **Events:** A public calendar listing webinars, seminars, and networking opportunities to drive attendance and engage the community.
*   **Contact:** Provides necessary contact information (form, email, phone, address) to make it easy for potential clients to reach out.
*   **Get Started:** The primary call-to-action page featuring a new enquiry form. It acts as the bridge between the website and operations, automatically feeding lead info into the Operations Portal.

### Phase 2: Oaktree Operations Portal (Internal)
The Oaktree Operations Portal is the internal command center for staff, providing a comprehensive suite of tools to manage the entire client lifecycle.

*   **Dashboard:** The initial login screen providing a real-time, high-level overview of key metrics. Aggregates data on lead flow, active customer status, and portfolio performance.
*   **Clients (Leads):** Manages prospective client interactions. Tracks leads from the "Get Started" form, allowing staff to log communications and nurture prospects.
*   **Customers:** The central repository for active client information. Staff can access profiles, manage accounts, and oversee the overall relationship.
*   **Portfolio:** Specialized tools for creating and monitoring investment portfolios. Allows advisors to track asset performance and generate client reports.
*   **Finance:** Handles financial tracking including invoicing, fee tracking, and commission management for transparent operations.
*   **Website (CMS):** Empowers non-technical staff to update the public website (copy, events, blog posts) without developers.
*   **Marketing:** Used for planning and tracking campaigns. Manage email distributions and segment audiences (leads vs. customers).
*   **Events (Internal):** For internal planning, managing logistics, and tracking attendee registrations from the website or portal.
*   **Staff:** Manages internal team info, directories, roles, contact details, and task assignments.
*   **Users:** Administrative module for controlling system access, permissions, and roles to ensure data security.

### Phase 3: Customer Portal (Client Facing)
A secure, personalized digital environment for Oaktree's existing clients.

*   **Dashboard:** The client's personalized home page offering a real-time snapshot of their financial standing, portfolio value, and recent performance.
*   **Documents:** A secure, private library for downloading statements, performance reports, tax documents, and contracts uploaded by Oaktree.
*   **Events:** Displays curated Oaktree events with options to view details and register directly.
*   **Settings:** Gives clients control over personal account details, passwords, and notification preferences.
